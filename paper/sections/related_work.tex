\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{AIGC Detection.}
The detection of AI-generated text has attracted intense research interest.
Supervised approaches train classifiers---typically BERT-based~\citep{devlin2019bert}---on corpora of human and machine-generated text~\citep{he2023mgtbench}.
Zero-shot methods exploit statistical properties of model outputs, such as probability curvature~\citep{mitchell2023detectgpt} or watermarking~\citep{kirchenbauer2023watermark}.
However, mounting evidence challenges the reliability of these approaches: \citet{sadasivan2023aigenerated} show that even simple paraphrasing can dramatically reduce detection accuracy, and cross-domain transfer remains fragile~\citep{he2023mgtbench}.
\citet{yang2024survey} provide a comprehensive survey highlighting the arms-race dynamics between generators and detectors.
Our work contributes to this discourse by demonstrating a principled, controllable method that exposes detector vulnerabilities.

\paragraph{Text Style Transfer.}
Style transfer in NLP aims to modify stylistic attributes (e.g., formality, sentiment, authorship) while preserving content~\citep{lyu2021style}.
Traditional approaches use variational autoencoders, back-translation, or retrieve-and-edit pipelines.
More recently, LLM-based rewriting has become a common strategy, though it often introduces its own detectable patterns.
Our work differs by operating in continuous embedding space rather than at the discrete token level, enabling smooth interpolation between styles.

\paragraph{Diffusion Models for Text.}
Diffusion models have achieved remarkable success in continuous domains (images, audio) and are increasingly applied to discrete text.
\citet{li2022diffusion} propose Diffusion-LM for controllable text generation by iterating in embedding space.
\citet{lou2024discrete} develop MDLM, which estimates data distribution ratios for discrete diffusion.
\langflow{} extends flow matching~\citep{lipman2023flow} to Chinese text generation with a DiT architecture~\citep{peebles2023scalable} and EDM-style noise schedules~\citep{karras2022elucidating}.
Our \styleshield{} builds on \langflow{} but introduces a fundamentally different task---\emph{conditional style transfer} rather than unconditional generation---by adding cross-attention conditioning and the SDEdit paradigm~\citep{meng2022sdedit}.

\paragraph{Adversarial Attacks on Text Classifiers.}
Adversarial NLP research has produced token-level perturbation methods such as TextFooler~\citep{jin2020bert}, which substitute words to flip classifier predictions.
While effective against specific classifiers, these methods operate at the lexical surface and lack the continuous controllability that \styleshield{} provides through its noise-level parameter~$\gamma$.
\citet{krishna2024paraphrasing} show that paraphrasing can evade detectors but note that retrieval-based defenses can partially counter this; our cross-attention conditioning approach goes beyond simple paraphrasing by performing semantics-guided generation in embedding space.
